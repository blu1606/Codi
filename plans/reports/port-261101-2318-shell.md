## Notes and concerns

- **The 8-character rule is not applied on sign-in.** The real app requires 8 characters, but the demo password `codi123` has 7. Sign-in checks only that a password was entered; register, reset and change still require 8.
- **Heatmap "activities" are derived at read time** from logins, enrolments, `learnerStudyDays` days with minutes (counted as course_view) and submissions (counted as lesson_complete). Quiz completions have no timestamps in the seed, so they are not counted.
- **The `githubAnalysis=timeout` flag waits the real 60 s.** Browser tests should allow for that.
