# AI grading assist: persisted job mechanism

## Outcome
"AI gợi ý điểm và nhận xét" on `/teach/grading/:id` now runs as a persisted job per submission (`db.aiGradingJobs`). The job moves through named stages with live progress, can be cancelled, retried and dismissed, and resumes after the lecturer navigates away or reloads. On success it shows a reviewable per-criterion draft. "Dùng bản nháp này" only fills the form (G5); it records `appliedAt`/`appliedBy` and never saves a grade. The grading list shows a per-submission badge: Đang chấm AI / Có gợi ý AI / Lỗi AI.

## Files
- `src/features/lecturer-ai/grading-draft-logic.js` (new): pure, deterministic `gradingResult(sub, assignment)`. It extends the old draft logic with a rationale and evidence quote per criterion, flags (Bài nộp quá ngắn, Thiếu file, Thiếu liên kết, Học viên ghi chú còn phần chưa làm), confidence, and coverage (`assessed`/`criteria`). A criterion the AI cannot assess gets `score: null` with a reason, never 0.
- `src/features/lecturer-ai/grading-jobs.js` (new): job store and simulated runner.
  - Timing is derived from `createdAt`/`startedAt`: queued for 0.6 s, then stages of 0.9, 0.8, 1.5 and 1.0 s. A reload therefore resumes at the right point, and an overdue job completes on the next tick.
  - Only stage transitions are written, so sync traffic stays at about 5 writes per job.
  - Each submission has at most one active job.
  - Scenario handling: `ratelimit` fails before running, `error` fails half-way through "Chấm từng tiêu chí", and `thinking` holds at 95% until cancelled.
  - The collection is created lazily when missing.
- `src/features/lecturer-ai/grading-ai.jsx` (rewritten): the job card behind `Codi.lecturerAI.gradingDraft` (same call site), plus the new `gradingBadge(submissionId)` and `gradingJobs` helpers. The legacy `getAiGradingDraft` is kept and now uses the shared logic.
- `src/features/lecturer-ai/lecturer-ai.css`: job card, stages, criteria, evidence and badge styles, with a `prefers-reduced-motion` override.
- `src/features/lecturer-ai/lecturer-ai-api.jsx`: one doc-comment line only.
- `src/features/lecturer/grading-page.jsx`: list badge via `Codi.lecturerAI?.gradingBadge?.(s.id)`, and the header comment.
- `server/seed-source/patches/65-ai-grading.js` (new): adds `db.aiGradingJobs` (additive) plus 2 succeeded jobs. `sub-001` is a ready suggestion. `sub-004` was applied before grading and has one unassessed criterion. Results were generated from `gradingResult`.

## Controller design guidance applied
1. Named stage list with pending, running, done, error and stopped states. A finished stage shows a one-line summary and its duration; the running stage shows text such as "Tiêu chí 2/4".
2. Each criterion's score appears as scoring reaches it. The total and feedback stay hidden while running, and the card says so ("Tổng điểm và nhận xét hiện khi chấm xong.").
3. On success the stages collapse into `<details>`: "4 bước · 4,8 giây · xem chi tiết".
4. Running state is shown in the card and the list badge; button labels never change.
5. An unassessed criterion shows "–/2" with its reason. Coverage reads "Đã chấm 3/4 tiêu chí", and the total counts only what the AI assessed.
6. The card carries a "Mô phỏng" badge with a tooltip (also readable by screen readers). Confidence is stated plainly: "Trung bình (57%), dựa trên lượng bằng chứng…".
7. Each state has one filled primary action: "Dùng bản nháp này" on success, "Thử lại" on failure.
8. Reduced motion turns off the spinner, reveal animation and progress-bar transition.

## Accessibility
- A persistent `aria-live="polite"` region announces queued, each stage, done, error and stopped.
- The progressbar has `aria-valuetext` (for example "53% — Chấm từng tiêu chí"), and the percentage is also shown as visible text.
- Each stage's state is exposed as sr-only text.
